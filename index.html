How it works

  1. Open your PDF. Drop it on the page or choose it. Your browser reads it locally; nothing is uploaded.
  2. Set up the stamp. Type a text like CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT, or pick a PNG or JPG logo. Choose opacity, angle, center or tiled, the layer and which pages to mark, and check the live preview.
  3. Apply and download. The stamp is drawn into the PDF on your device and saved as name_watermarked.pdf. The rest of the page content is left as it was.

Questions, answered

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. PDF Watermark Stamper runs entirely in your browser. Your PDF and your logo are read into memory on your device, stamped with JavaScript, and never sent to a server. You can disconnect from the internet and it keeps working.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license, with no account, no daily limits, no paid tier for image stamps and no ads. If it saves you a paid subscription, you can say thanks on Ko-fi.

Can I stamp text in Hebrew, Arabic, Chinese or with emoji?

The built-in PDF fonts (Helvetica, Times, Courier) only cover Latin letters. When your text has other characters, the tool offers a button that draws the text into a transparent image with your system fonts and stamps that image instead, so every character shows up.

What is the difference between over and under the content?

Over puts the stamp on top of everything, so it is always visible. Under paints it first and lets the page draw over it, which keeps text easy to read. If a page has a solid white background (common in scanned PDFs), an under stamp is hidden by it, so use over for scans.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the page is cached by a service worker, so it loads without a connection. You can also install it from your browser's menu as an app.
